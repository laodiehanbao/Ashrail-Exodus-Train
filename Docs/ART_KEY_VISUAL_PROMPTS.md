## Final Prompt

```text
Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: vertical 9:16 mobile game key visual concept art for Ashrail Exodus Train, no text and no UI
Primary request: Create a polished concept art key visual for a Douyin-style mobile mini-game about an apocalyptic modular armored exodus train, focused on loot-box rewards and train growth.
Scene/backdrop: elevated broken railway through an apocalyptic ash wasteland, broken viaduct, dust, sparks, smoke, distant ruined city silhouettes, red dusk sky, minimal readable background.
Subject: a chunky modular armored exodus train thundering forward from lower foreground toward upper distance in a dynamic 2.5D three-quarter perspective. The locomotive is the clear hero subject with rusted steel plating, reinforced cowcatcher, glowing furnace core, strong wheels, bolted armor plates, and readable train silhouette. Show exactly three clear train sections: locomotive, combat carriage with mounted cannon and armor sockets, resource/core carriage with supply crates and glowing energy core.
Loot focus: an oversized glowing loot chest is opening in the foreground near the train, bursting with warm golden light, coins, gear fragments, equipment shards, weapon parts, blueprint pieces, and a few rare item beams. The reward burst should be the brightest commercial hook and should guide the eye back to the train.
Style/medium: high-quality stylized game concept art, 2.5D mobile game key art, premium but readable, detailed but not cluttered, thumbnail-readable at small phone size.
Composition/framing: vertical 9:16, train occupies about 70 percent of the image, centered slightly lower, strong silhouette, high contrast, no extreme perspective distortion, no tiny distant train. Keep important elements inside the central 70 percent for mobile cropping.
Lighting/mood: warm golden loot-box explosion as main light, ember red furnace glow, orange rim light on train edges, small cyan energy accents only on module sockets and core, ash-gray sky and controlled red dusk danger mood. Satisfying progression and survival, not horror.
Color palette: dark iron gray, coal black, rust red, ember orange, high-saturation gold reward light, tiny cyan energy accents; avoid dominant purple or neon cyberpunk look.
Constraints: no text, no logo, no UI, no buttons, no health bars, no readable writing, no giant character, no oversized monster. Small hostile silhouettes may appear only as background pressure and must not compete with the train or loot chest.
Avoid: text, logo, title, watermark, UI panels, buttons, icons, menus, health bars, damage numbers, speech bubbles, subtitles, readable writing, brand marks, modern bullet train, clean sci-fi spaceship, cyberpunk city, fantasy castle, cute cartoon train, realistic photo, low-resolution, blurry, muddy composition, overcluttered scene, flat side view, empty background, human face close-up, gore, excessive blood, horror poster style, pure monochrome palette, dominant purple gradient, soft bokeh orbs, abstract shapes, duplicated locomotive, broken perspective, deformed wheels, impossible rails, tiny train, over-detailed realistic long train, dark unreadable smoke.
```

## Review Notes

- Accepted: readable armored train, modular cannon carriage, foreground loot burst, strong gold reward lighting.
- Watch next iteration: reduce background enemy silhouettes if using this as an app icon base.
